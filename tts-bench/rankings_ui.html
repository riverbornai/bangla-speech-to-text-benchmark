How to read this

Built on human ears.

Every sentence was synthesised by all seven models, shuffled per sentence so raters never knew which voice they were hearing, then scored 1–5 for naturalness and pronunciation. A model's score is its mean rating.

At Riverborn, we build AI you can trust.
Read with care
  • Small categories (e.g. Long-form, 6 sentences) swing easily. Treat gaps under ~0.3 as noise.
  • Ties are shown as “=”. Overall pools every rating, so busier categories weigh more.
  • Category descriptions are inferred from each category's test sentences.
